Forty day challenge

  1. Confess, confess, confess. God will forgive... God will change our lives.

  2. Keep using the rules daily and especially confession. Never stop.

  3. A verse a day for forty days — let God change your life a morning at a time.

Forty days is not a test. Nothing here is earned — God forgave you when you asked Him. The forty days are simply forty mornings of the three rules, said again. Each morning there is one verse, the same for everyone who holds this card, and the three rules under it. Read the verse. Use the rules. If you miss a morning, there is nothing to make up — start again where you are. On the fortieth day nothing ends. The rules are for every day after that one.
