Brother Daniel — the interview sheet · Ang sheet ng panayam

Ten prompts for ONE sitting (3r4l.org, phase 2 rung 1 — DRAFT 2026-10-08). The prompts are for the conversation, never the page's headings: his words land verbatim, in his order, under at most three heads that are his own phrases.   1. How did the cards begin — what happened the day you gave the first one?Paano nagsimula ang mga kard — ano ang nangyari sa araw na ibinigay mo ang una?

  5. Tell me about a day it was hard to keep giving.Ikuwento mo ang isang araw na mahirap magpatuloy sa pagbibigay.
  6. What have you seen happen because of a card?Ano ang nakita mong nangyari dahil sa isang kard?
