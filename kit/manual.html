3r4l.org

Nothing on your card changes unless you choose it. This page is the one addition: an address a reader can open after the card is in their hand.

  1. The address. 3r4l.org — short enough to print beside anything. It opens the card's own words in Tagalog, with English behind one small word at the top.
  2. The QR and the address, if you want them. The kit holds the QR as a picture (SVG and PNG) and the address as plain text, for your printer to set into the card's own design on your next run — your card is printed on both sides, so there is no blank side to stamp. Neither is required; the card stands on its own.
  3. Printing more. 3r4l.org/card opens your card as the print file; 3r4l.org/kit opens the sheet of cards for A4 and Letter paper with cut marks. These two doors open once you say yes to the card's download. The Tagalog page prints the English card unless you print a Tagalog one.
  4. Sharing from a phone. The what-next page has a Share button that opens the phone's own share sheet. Where a phone has none, five plain links stand in its place: Messenger and Viber first, then text, WhatsApp and email. The message is the headline and the address, nothing else.
  5. The daily verse. One of forty, the same for everyone that day, each from a Bible text named under it. Day 1 is the day the address first goes out on your cards — tell Steve that day and the page is set to it in minutes. Before that day every reader sees day 1.
  6. A testimonial. Only a real one, with the giver's consent on your paper (first name or initials, no surname, no photo). Send it to Steve; there is no form on the page. A row comes off the page on the giver's ask.
  7. If something is wrong. Tell Steve. And before the address goes on your next run of cards: from your own phone on a Philippine SIM, open 3r4l.org, tap one verse's link to Knowing Jesus, and scan the QR once.
  8. Nothing is tracked. The page sets no cookie, runs no tracker and asks for nothing — no name, no email, no location. There is nothing to sign up to.

Written as an addition to a ministry already running. Your words stay your words.
